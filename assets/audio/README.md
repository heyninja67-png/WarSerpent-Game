# Original audio

All seven WAV files were synthesized specifically for War Serpent.io by the original, reproducible synthesizer in `tools/generate_audio.py`. No third-party recordings or samples are included.

Audio is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0-1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Attribution to Emnio is appreciated but not required.

22,050 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM. Sound effects are mono; `ambient.wav` is a 12-second stereo loop. Use low music volume (about -18 dB); the loop was intentionally composed as quiet sci-fi atmosphere.
